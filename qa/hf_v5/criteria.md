# Criteria — 01 Hair Force gameplay v5 (20 s, 4:3, gameplay screencast)
1. Spec: 1440×1080, 30 fps, 20 ± 0.2 s, bt709, AAC stereo; decodes clean.
2. Hook: an event (thrust puff/motion) in f0–f15, a readable wow (bonus tie gate pass + BONUS ×2) within the first 2 s.
3. Gameplay screencast rule (memory gameplay-trailer-screenrec): only game + HUD, game camera, no added titles/logos/endcard beyond the game's own UI.
4. Car readable and large: car ≥ ~18% of frame width, never cut by frame edge while alive; crash visible in frame.
5. No IP: no Tesla/SpaceX/Trump names, logos or faces; hair-only caricature; car is an original design.
6. Seams: 2 cuts (≈f60, ≈f482). No flash/ghost frames; score jumps are only at cuts (hook→start is the declared flash-forward, start→end jumps forward).
7. No repeated/judder frames (machine list empty), no static hold > 0.8 s except the game-over card ≤ 1.5 s.
8. HUD text: score always on screen, not cut, ≥ 18 px; BAD HAIR DAY card readable.
9. Ending payoff: crash with sparks + spin, camera follows, BAD HAIR DAY with score.
10. Sound: −14 ±1 LUFS, TP ≤ −1 dBTP, thrust/score/bonus/crash SFX present on events (measured from event log, not listened).
11. Music from NON IP library (The Pump), no silence ≥ 0.5 s.
